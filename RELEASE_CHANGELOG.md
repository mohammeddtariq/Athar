# Introducing Al-Amanah - الأَمَنَة: (Release 1.0.7-beta)

> *«أَمَنَة لِعَقْلٍ لَا يَهْدَأ، يَلْقَى فِيهَا بَيْنَ كُلِّ آيَةٍ سَلَامًا مَعَ كَلَامِ الله»*  
> *"Al-Amanah: A sanctuary for a restless mind, bringing stillness between every verse with the words of Allah."*

# What changed?

### ✨ New Features

- **Al-Amanah (الأَمَنَة) — An All-New Quranic Audio System**:  
  An entirely new dedicated audio sanctuary inside Athar designed for immersive, distraction-free Quran listening. It introduces:
  - **Curated Thematic Playlists**: Handpicked listening journeys tailored for deep focus, calm contemplation, morning/evening remembrance, and solace.
  - **New Renowned Reciters**: Choose from a blessed lineup of legendary voices, including Sheikh Mohamed Siddiq Al-Minshawi, Sheikh Abdul Basit Abdul Samad, Sheikh Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary, Sheikh Mishary Alafasy, Sheikh Maher Al-Muaiqly, Sheikh Abdur-Rahman As-Sudais, Sheikh Sa'ud Ash-Shuraym, and Sheikh Abu Bakr Al-Shatri.
  - **Background Playback & Media Controls**: Full system `MediaSession` integration with lock screen album art, persistent notification controls, and seamless multitasking.
  - **Hardware Audio Output Switching**: Switch instantly between your phone's built-in speaker and connected Bluetooth earbuds/headphones without pausing or buffering.
  - **Fullscreen Ambient Player**: Features customizable sleep timers, playback speed controls, interactive verse syncing, and fluid swipe-to-dismiss gestures.
  - **Authentic Listening Telemetry**: Real, privacy-respecting listening history that powers your Monthly Replay with zero fake or simulated stats.

- **Smart Jumu'ah Reminders**: Thoughtful Friday prayer reminders that automatically recognize the blessed day to help you prepare with prayer, Quran recitation, and blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ.

---

### 🐛 Bug Fixes & Improvements

- **Audio Output Routing**: Resolved an issue where audio would not switch between Bluetooth earbuds and internal speakers.
- **Infinite Buffering Fix**: Fixed indefinite loading on initial playback with robust streaming fallbacks and safety watchdog.
- **Visual Artistry**: Replaced placeholder artwork with authentic 16-lobed Islamic *Shamsah* illumination medallions across all playlist covers.
- **Interface Polish**: Modernized the Al-Amanah floating squircle dock button and decluttered the home top bar with an icon-only settings action.
- **General Stability**: Performance optimizations, reduced memory footprint, and refined UI animations throughout the app.
